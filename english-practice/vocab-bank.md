# Vocabulary Bank

> 累積所有 session 挖出的詞與片語。最舊的在最上面，定期複習。

## 2026-05-04 (daily-standup)

- **wrap up** — 收尾、完成。例：I wrapped up a few things yesterday.
- **ship** — 把功能做完並上線（前端最常用）。例：I shipped the virtual tag feature.
- **knock out** — 快速做完一批小事 / 修一堆小 bug。例：I'll knock out the bugs QA flagged.
- **a 1:1** — 一對一會議（名詞，前面加 a / had）。例：I had a 1:1 with my team lead.
- **dig into** — 深入調查（bug、log）。例：Let me dig into it and get back to you.
- **get back to you** — 之後回覆你（站會神句）。例：I'll get back to you by EOD.
- **EOD** — end of day。例：I'll have an answer by EOD.
- **As for X, ...** — 切換話題到 X。例：As for the mobile crash, ...
- **evaluate / compare libraries** — 比較套件選型（取代 survey package）。例：I'll evaluate a few drag-and-drop libraries.
- **spike on** — 花一小段時間做技術調研，看可不可行。例：I'll spike on Framer Motion this morning.
- **on my plate** — 我手上要做的事。例：Two things on my plate today.
- **flag** — （QA / reviewer）指出問題。例：The bugs QA flagged in the e2e tests.
- **circle back to** — 等等再回來處理。例：I'll circle back to the API piece after lunch.
- **thanks for the heads-up** — 謝謝提醒（取代 "thanks for your information"，站會 / Slack 必備）。
- **good call** — 你說得對 / 好建議。例：Good call, I hadn't thought of that.
- **ping** — Slack 戳人 / 訊息找人。例：I'll ping Mike right after standup.
- **reach out to** — 主動聯繫某人（比 ask 自然）。例：I'll reach out to him later.
- **loop someone in** — 把某人拉進對話 / 把他們加到 thread。例：Let me loop in the backend team.
- **heads up** — 提醒一下（名詞 / 動詞片語）。例：Heads up — staging will be down at 3pm.
- **be out / be offline / be AFK** — 不在線上、不在崗位（取代 "I will leave X hours"）。AFK = Away From Keyboard。
- **No blockers (from my side)** — 站會結尾標準回答，「我這邊沒有卡住」。
- **Quick FYI** — FYI = For Your Information，「順便講一下」。
- **on my end / from my side** — 在我這邊。例：Everything looks good on my end.
- **save me a ton of time** — 幫我省一堆時間（誇張但很常用的口語）。
